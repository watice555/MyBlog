# 《拉兰古墨文明简史》节选

和其他文明一样，拉兰古墨文明也拥有基于天体运动的日、月、年等时间单位。不过，在日常生活中更常使用的，却是费奥瓦和赛瓦迪斯：一个赛瓦迪斯等于33.6个费奥瓦，而一年约等于52.18个赛瓦迪斯。

“为什么要采用换算如此不便的时间单位？”你或许会产生这样的疑问。

答案要追溯到文明尚处雏形的年代。

当时，由于能源开发技术还十分落后，许多个体无法持续进行活动，其活动与休止受到两种不同周期的制约。这两个周期后来逐渐成为安排活动、约定事务的通用时间单位。

随着文明的发展，最初的严格限制逐渐退出历史舞台，但由此形成的计时习惯却保留了下来，一直沿用至今。

——《拉兰古墨文明简史》节选

Like other civilizations, the Lalangumo civilization also developed units of time based on the movements of celestial bodies, such as days, months, and years. In everyday life, however, two other units were far more commonly used: the Faiovar and the Sevadis. One Sevadis was equivalent to 33.6 Faiovars, while a year contained approximately 52.18 Sevadises.

"Why adopt such inconveniently proportioned units of time?" you might wonder.
The answer lies in the earliest days of the civilization.

At the time, energy-harvesting technology was still primitive, and many individuals were unable to remain continuously active. Their periods of activity and dormancy were governed by two distinct cycles. Over time, these cycles gradually became the standard units for scheduling activities and coordinating affairs.

As the civilization advanced, the strict limitations of its early days gradually faded into history. Yet the conventions of timekeeping born from those limitations endured, remaining in use to this day.

— Excerpt from *A Brief History of the Lalangumo Civilization*
